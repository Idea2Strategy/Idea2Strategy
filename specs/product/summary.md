# Product summary

Idea2Strategy is a server-operated virtual US stock and ETF trading-bot SaaS. Users build deterministic strategies visually, launch immutable strategy versions, operate simulated bots, inspect decisions and official ledgers, and compare bots only inside rooms under locked and objectively calculated rules.
